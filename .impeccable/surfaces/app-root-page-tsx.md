## Scope

Whole app: sign-in and sign-up (the public landing), dashboard, stock pages, portfolio, trade history, watchlist, search, order ticket, AI review. Mode: Operate (auth pages lean Persuade inside the same system). Code-led: no image generation.

## Audience and job

Learners practising with stakes removed, and evaluators opening the demo. Task: read how the account moved and why, look up a stock, place a paper order with a clear preview, understand concentration.

## Direction contract

THESIS: StockLens is a dark trading desk with the hierarchy of a premium fintech product: one gold action, market colour only for market meaning, and every figure tabular. It refuses neon-terminal costume and generic card-grid dashboards.

OWN-WORLD: The owner's original palette, pinned: canvas #050505, surface #141414, raised #212328, lines #30333a, gold #e8ba40 for the single primary action and current place, gain green and loss #ff495b only with a sign and an arrow. Geist for UI, Geist Mono for tickers, tabular figures for money. Bordered sheets, no decorative gradients, shadows only on overlays. The original logo and images stay.

STORY: The visitor sees total equity, today's move and performance, understands allocation and concentration from computed insights, opens a stock, previews an order's cost and resulting cash and shares, places it, and sees the confirmation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (logo, Dashboard/Watchlist/Portfolio/History, search with Ctrl K, account) over a watchlist price strip. Left two thirds: total equity with today's change, Positions and gold Trade, the P&L chart with 1W/1M/3M/YTD/All, a four-stat row. Right third: Insights from the positions, then Watchlist movers with diverging move bars. Allocation bar below.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) "maintain the dark colour theme we previously had", "Keep the previous logos and images", "sleek, high agency and professional", with Linear, Stripe, Ramp, Robinhood and Bloomberg named as the craft bar.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
